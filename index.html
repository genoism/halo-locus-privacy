Privacy Policy

This policy describes Halo Locus, the Android game from Toaster on Fire Studios. It was last updated on November 8, 2026.

Questions about this policy can be sent to genoism@gmail.com.

What the game stores on your phone

Halo Locus keeps your voyage on the phone. That includes the ship, crew, inventory, notes, and progress. The game also keeps a short local log of gameplay events, such as whether a sign-in succeeded. Those files stay on the device until you uninstall the game or clear its storage. If you use Android backup, Google may include that local save in the phone’s backup.

The game can show notifications for things that finish while you are away, such as travel or mining. It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. It does not show ads.

Google sign-in and cloud save

Sign-in is optional. If you sign in with Google Play Games, Google receives the sign-in and Halo Locus can store a copy of your voyage in Play Games saved games, tied to your Play Games player. The game uses the name from that account so it can show who is signed in. If you play without signing in, the voyage stays only on the phone.

You can sign out inside the game. To remove a cloud copy, delete the Play Games saved data for Halo Locus from your Google account, or email the address above and ask for help.

Gameplay analytics

The game can send gameplay event names to Google Firebase Analytics, such as the result of a sign-in attempt. Those events are used to understand whether the game is working. Firebase is operated by Google and is covered by Google’s own privacy policy. The same notes are also kept in the local log on the phone.

What we do not do

Toaster on Fire Studios does not sell your information. The game does not ask you to create a separate Halo Locus account. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Halo Locus is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
